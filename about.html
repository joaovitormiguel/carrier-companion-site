About

Twenty years in the building.

Carrier Companion is built by 3PL Systems, a transportation software company that has spent twenty years building BrokerWare for freight brokers. Its asset-based broker customers helped shape a new kind of TMS built for the asset side. The people who built it have been in transportation software for two decades. The people who helped shape it run trucks.

Startup energy. Veteran hands.

A small team that moves fast and answers the phone, and a product built on current technology from the ground up. New product, not a new company.
