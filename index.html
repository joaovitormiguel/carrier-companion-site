The whiteboard goes back to being a whiteboard.

Most fleets run on a whiteboard, a handful of spreadsheets, a shared calendar or whatever the ops manager keeps in their head. It works, as long as the one person who knows it all is at their desk.

Carrier Companion puts the map, the board, the loads and trips, the drivers and equipment, the customers, documents, rates and invoices all in one place.

Fewer tabs. Fewer spreadsheets. Fewer things only one person knows.

Nothing out of view.

One platform for the people who run the fleet.

Dispatch and trip planning

Assign the day in one pass. Loads into trips, trips to drivers, planned once on one board with a record of every change.

Live tracking

Know before it's asked. Where's the truck, when it delivers, whether the POD came in. The answer is on the screen when the customer calls, so the call is short.

Quote to invoice

From the quote to the invoice. Rates set once, truckload and LTL. The POD lands on the load. The invoice goes out from the same record, and driver pay settles from it too.

Driver app

One app in the cab. What the driver marks on his phone shows up on your board. Pickups, deliveries, photos, signed PODs.

Integrations

The tools you already run, connected. ELDs, fuel cards, tracking, your customers' EDI. Set up your way, all in the platform.

Built for fleets. Not a trucking tab on a broker's system.

Most asset TMSs are a brokerage system with trucking stapled on. Carrier Companion was designed as its own platform, for the asset side, and syncs with broker systems only where data needs to move. If you run a brokerage and a fleet, each division gets the tool that was made for it.

Why it was built separately →

Twenty years in the building.

Carrier Companion is a new product from the team at 3PL Systems who have built transportation software for twenty years. It exists because 3PL Systems customers who also run trucks kept asking for a version built for the asset side. Those asset-based customers helped shape the platform.

FAQ

Each division on its own tool. The brokerage on BrokerWare, the fleet on Carrier Companion, connected where the data has to move.
